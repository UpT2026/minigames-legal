Privacy Policy

Minigames for iPhone · Effective November 3, 2026

In short: Minigames does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your scores and game progress stay on your iPhone.

Who we are

Minigames (“the app”) is developed and published by Mohammed Baydi (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, email zelatmoha@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not create an account for you. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and no third-party SDKs.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • best scores, number of runs and time played for each game;
  • your favorite games and recently played games;
  • saved progress for games that support it;
  • your settings (sound effects and haptics).

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time in Settings › Reset All Progress, or for a single game on its page. Deleting the app deletes it too. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, this data is included in that backup and handled by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Internet use

Every game is included in the app and runs from your device. Games are not allowed to connect to the internet while you play. The only time the app opens a web page is when you tap a link yourself, for example a game’s open-source project or license in the Credits section. Those pages open in Safari and are covered by the privacy policies of the websites you visit.

Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated information such as crash reports and App Store statistics. This is controlled by you and Apple, and it does not identify you to us. See Apple’s privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Minigames is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not have any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever needed to collect information, this policy would be updated before that version is released.
